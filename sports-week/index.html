One week, both apps

A parent sets it up once. Here's where each piece comes from:

  1. Both schedulesThe parent pastes each app's calendar link once. It's free, allowed, and updates on its own. This demo reads the same calendar format.
  2. TeamSnap RSVPsThe parent taps "Sign in with TeamSnap" (TeamSnap's official login). Their RSVPs show here, and a tap answers.
  3. SportsEngine RSVPsSportsEngine has no sign-in for outside apps, so one tap opens that event in SportsEngine.
  4. To-dos from team emailsThe parent has team emails forwarded to Sports Week. AI pulls out what to bring, wear, pay or sign, and links each item to the game. Tap one to see the email it came from.
  5. Clash checkFlags times when two kids need to be in two places at once. It works from the merged schedule, so it needs no extra data.

✨ AI turned the highlighted words into to-dos. The demo uses sample emails.
