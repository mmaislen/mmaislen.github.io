The problem

AI pilots are easy to start. Getting people to use them is the hard part.

Most teams can stand up a demo in a week. Far fewer turn it into something customers rely on. The gap is rarely the model. It's choosing the right problem, designing an experience people trust, getting many teams to agree, and landing the change with the people who have to use it.

That gap is where I work. AI speeds up every individual piece of building a product. Someone still has to connect the pieces, decide what's worth building, and see it through to adoption.
