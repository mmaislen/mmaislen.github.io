My work

A few things I've shipped

Microsoft Teams

Millions of people

Copilot and AI agents, in reach in every meeting

How it happened

Copilot and AI agents were already in Teams, but people had to hunt for them. So I redesigned the meeting controls at the center of every meeting to put Copilot, Facilitator, Interpreter and AI notes one click away. People used AI more, ran into less friction, and told us they liked it.

Microsoft Mesh

Enterprise first

Work accounts on VR headsets, adopted by Accenture

How it happened

Companies wanted employees in immersive meetings, but headsets didn't work with their work accounts. I led the device strategy that let people sign in to Meta Quest with Microsoft 365 and use PowerPoint, Word and Loop in 3D. Accenture used it to onboard all of its new employees.

Nordstrom.com

$2M–$5M+ conversion lift

Experimentation and growth on one of retail's busiest sites

How it happened

On a site this busy, small changes add up fast. I ran A/B tests on the header and navigation that lifted conversion across the whole site, launched an Android banner that tripled app downloads, and led site updates for a record-breaking Anniversary Sale.

Microsoft Teams

Industry first

Sign language detection AI, shipped at scale

How it happened

Meeting tools only noticed people who spoke out loud, so people who sign were easy to miss. As lead PM, I took sign language detection from idea to launch so signers get the spotlight too. It's now the foundation for crediting Deaf and hard-of-hearing participants correctly in captions, transcripts and AI summaries.

AI enablement

~200 people trained

From nervous to building their own AI workflows

How it happened

When I started training colleagues on an AI agent pilot, I expected technical questions. What I got was nerves. So I skipped the architecture and showed one plain-English example: “Schedule a meeting with my manager at 10am tomorrow.” About 200 people later, teams were building their own workflows.
